Teensy Keyboard

Overview

I harvested the keyboard from an obsolete Chromebook 11e and followed a guide to make it a USB device. As simple as that sounds, it was quite the experience and giving new purpose to an old keyboard was extremely fun. I used the Teensy 4.0 microcontoller to map out keyboard matrix pins and then followed the guide to make the Teensy act as the USB device which would send those interpreted keys to the computer. This involved tearing down an old Chromebook, finding a compatible FPC connector and soldering it to a PCB.
Although I did not do everything in this project and mostly followed a guide and recieved micro-soldering help, I did keenly understand the process and the complexities involved.
